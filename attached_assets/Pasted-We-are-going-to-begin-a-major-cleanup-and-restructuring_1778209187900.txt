We are going to begin a major cleanup and restructuring of JORTRADE. Before building additional features, I want to simplify, modernize, and reposition the platform into a cleaner AI-powered trading intelligence experience.

Primary goals:

* Reduce clutter
* Improve navigation flow
* Simplify language
* Reorganize tabs/pages
* Remove unnecessary complexity
* Improve professionalism and scalability
* Reposition away from “signal selling” and more toward market intelligence, education, analytics, and workflow support

IMPORTANT:
JORTRADE should NOT be framed as an auto-trading, financial advisory, or copy-trading platform. We are moving toward:

* AI market intelligence
* conversational trading analysis
* breakout and momentum scanning
* market structure interpretation
* educational trade planning
* trader workflow tools

We will start with a full page-by-page review and cleanup.

Initial priorities:

1. Audit every page and component
2. Remove dead/outdated/redundant UI elements
3. Reorganize sidebar/tab order for a more logical trader workflow
4. Simplify terminology across the platform
5. Replace aggressive execution language with safer market-intelligence language
6. Improve overall visual consistency and user experience

Examples of terminology changes:

* “Buy Signal” → “Setup Focus”
* “Entry” → “Confirmation Zone”
* “Target” → “Potential Reaction Area”
* “Stop Loss” → “Structure Risk Level”
* “Execution” → “Market Conditions”
* Remove “BUY NOW,” “SELL,” or direct execution wording

Core public-facing areas should focus on:

* Biddie Chat
* Market Search
* Breakout Scanner
* Market Intelligence Dashboard
* Watchlists
* Trader Journal
* Analytics & Market Context

JORTRADE Futures / execution automation will remain separate and private for internal use only.

I want the platform to feel:

* modern
* intelligent
* conversational
* premium
* clean
* easy to understand
* institutional-inspired without overwhelming users

Before making major code changes:

* estimate impact
* identify affected files
* avoid touching unrelated systems
* prioritize modular cleanup
* keep changes scoped and organized

We will tackle this one section at a time starting with navigation, sidebar order, and terminology cleanup.
