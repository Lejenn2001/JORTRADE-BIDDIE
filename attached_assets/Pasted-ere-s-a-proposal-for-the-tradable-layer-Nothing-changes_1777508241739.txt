ere's a proposal for the tradable layer. Nothing changes in signal generation/scoring/outcomes — this is a parallel system that consumes signals and adds tradability metadata, contract picks, entry/exit rules, and a tradability label. Implementation waits for your approval.

Architecture (one-line summary)A new module tradablePlan runs immediately after a signal is generated (and on-demand for replay/backtest). It reads the signal but never writes back to it. Its output lives in two new tables — signal_tradable_plan and tradable_executions — joined into the Paper Trades UI by signal_id. Live signal flow, alerts, Biddie, and scoring are untouched.

"Tradable vs directional only" classifierThis is the most important piece — most of yesterday's losses come from signals that were directionally accurate but mechanically untradable.

New field: tradability: "A" | "B" | "C" | "skip" written to signal_tradable_plan.tradability_grade.

Computation (independent of signal grade):

tradability_score (0–100) =0.30 * contract_quality      // best available contract's spread + liquidity + delta fit

0.25 * setup_quality         // signal type + market context (trend day vs chop)

0.20 * timing_quality        // time-of-day, distance to next event/earnings

0.15 * iv_environment        // current IV vs historical (low IV = better entries)

0.10 * historical_replay     // rolling 30-day replay P&L of similar signalsBuckets:

A (≥ 75): high-confidence tradable — show big green "Tradable A" badge, recommend Primary contractB (60–74): tradable with caveats — show yellow badge, recommend Backup contract or smaller sizeC (45–59): directional only — show grey badge "Watch Only", suggest paper trade or passSkip (< 45): badge "Untradable — [reason: spread too wide / no liquid contracts / IV too high / earnings in 2 days]"Stays parallel to existing signal grade:A signal can be Signal Grade = A (the directional thesis is strong) but Tradability = Skip (no contract has tight enough spread). These are now two separate axes, and that's the whole point of the layer.

Bonus benefit: the replay/backtest panel becomes vastly more useful — you can filter eligible past signals by tradability grade and see "what would have happened if I had only paper-traded A-tradables?"
