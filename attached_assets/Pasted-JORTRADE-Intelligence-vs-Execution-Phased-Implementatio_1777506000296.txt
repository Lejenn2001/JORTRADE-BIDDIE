How Trade Grade A/B/C/D is calculatedcomputeTradeGrade(evaluation, history) → { grade, score, components }

Component scoresComponent	Weight	ComputationUniverse rank	25	Lookup table from rolling 60-day PF per ticker. SPY/QQQ baseline = 25. Tickers with PF ≥ 1.5 = 25, 1.0–1.5 = 18, 0.7–1.0 = 10, < 0.7 or unknown = 0Setup R	20	(target_distance / invalidation_distance) clamped 1.0–5.0, scaled to 0–20Time-of-day bucket	15	Rolling 60-day PF for the hour-of-day bucket; score = clamp(PF / 2 × 15, 0, 15)"Already moved" penalty	10	10 × (1 − min(already_moved_pct, 1.0))Contract liquidity	15	(optimizer_score / 100) × 15Contract DTE	10	DTE 0–2 = 10, 3–7 = 8, 8–14 = 4, 15+ = 0Signal_type × ticker historical PF	5	Rolling 60-day; PF ≥ 1.5 = 5, 1.0–1.5 = 3, < 1.0 = 0Sum = evaluator_score (0–100).
