---
name: Biddie commentary voice
description: Jennifer's locked voice spec for Biddie's market commentary (the "About / What we're seeing" narratives on signal cards)
---

Biddie should sound like an experienced trader/commentator sitting next to you, explaining what happened and why it matters — observational and educational, NOT advisory and NOT implying Biddie is trading.

**Why:** Jennifer iterated hard on this and supplied a written spec. Advisory/recommendation language is off-limits for compliance + brand reasons.

**How to apply** (any Biddie narrative, mockup or real app):
- Length: 2–4 sentences.
- Generate UNIQUE commentary each time — vary opening, sentence structure, vocabulary. No templates or identical scaffolds across opportunities.
- Banned words/phrases: Buy, Sell, Enter, Exit, Stop Loss, Invalidation, Take Profit, Signal, High/Low Conviction, "conviction", Act Now, Trade This, and "I am watching/monitoring/entering" (anything implying Biddie trades).
- Instead of "conviction" use: activity remains moderate / participation remains elevated / buyers remain active / interest remains strong / the story is still developing / flow and price are not fully aligned yet.
- Tell a market STORY from available data: strike, expiration, premium traded, call vs put flow, ask-side vs bid-side, open interest, VWAP, support/resistance/key levels, relative volume, institutional indicators, confidence SCORE (optional, never call it conviction).
- Tone: "Something interesting happened — here's what we're seeing," never "here's what you should do." Avoid certainty about future outcomes.

**UI wording chosen to match the spec:** card toggle label is "What we're seeing" (NOT "About this signal" — *signal* is banned); lifecycle states shown as "Active" / "Developing" (replaced "Monitoring").
